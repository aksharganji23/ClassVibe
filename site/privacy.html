Privacy policy

ClassVibe is built so that your data stays on your phone.

What ClassVibe stores

Your timetable, exams, holidays, attendance marks and settings, stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

What leaves your phone

  • Update check (optional): the app asks GitHub for the latest release of ClassVibe. No personal data is sent. You can turn this off in Settings → Updates.
  • Phone cloud backup (optional): if enabled in Settings and in your Android backup settings, Android may back up app data to your own Google account. You can turn it off in Settings → Backup.
  • Things you share yourself: QR codes, CSV, calendar (.ics) or backup files go only where you send them.

What ClassVibe does not do

  • No accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.
  • No camera, location or contacts permission. Photos and QR scans go through Android's own camera and Google's code scanner; text recognition runs on your device.
